# Story Arc And Level Beats

This document is the story-design source for the first course and later dual-dino expansion. It turns pedagogy into cute, concrete level moments so the app does not drift into abstract drills.

## Core Story Promise

The child is not "doing exercises." The child is helping tiny dinosaurs build homes in space with piano notes.

One-line story:

> 原创小星芽龙乘飞船来到月亮，从一座还没封好的小前哨开始寻找新家。孩子用钢琴音符帮它搭家、过桥、找到会呼吸的花园和三角龙咚咚，最后把两个安全住处连成会唱歌的大家园。

Teaching translation:

- a piano key is where the note energy lives;
- a note name tells the dinosaur which energy to use;
- a staff position is a jump spot or bridge tile;
- a correct sound makes the world change;
- a wrong sound gives a gentle clue, not punishment.

Every level beat must also pass `33_GAME_TEACHING_PACING_AND_MOTIVATION_CONTRACT.md`: one visible story problem, one new teaching axis, one necessary music action, an immediate world response, a bounded repair, a permanent session consequence, and a natural rest point. A cute scene without a necessary learning action is not a finished story beat; a correct exercise without a visible story consequence is not a finished game beat.

## Character Plan

### Character 1: Xingya

Default Chinese name: `星芽`

Species and body plan:

- canonical fictional species: `星芽龙`;
- anatomy is informed by a small agile bipedal ornithopod, with a balancing tail and readable forelimbs;
- not a Tyrannosaurus, raptor, sauropod, triceratops, or a claimed real-species reconstruction;
- requires air and pressure, so exterior moon and open-space scenes use a complete soft pressure suit rather than a helmet alone.

Role:

- first companion in Chapter 1;
- small, bright, curious, likes jumping;
- represents early right-hand/high-register discovery later, but is not introduced as a technical label at first.

Design notes:

- silhouette should be simple and readable at small size;
- expressions: curious, pointing, listening, tiny stumble, happy jump, celebration;
- avoid looking like any existing dinosaur IP, superhero, anime mascot, or toy brand.

Teaching personality:

- "I found a small sound."
- "This key wakes the floor."
- "Can you help me jump to that note?"

### Character 2: Dongdong

Default Chinese name: `咚咚`

Role:

- later companion from the low planet;
- round, steady, warm, strong;
- represents left-hand/low-register/bass foundation later.

Design notes:

- chubby triceratops silhouette;
- strong feet, gentle eyes, small star tools;
- feels stable and capable, never dumb or slow.

Teaching personality:

- "My planet needs steady sounds."
- "Low notes can hold the bridge."
- "I can carry the big pieces."

### Naming Customization

The app may offer a light naming moment after the first chapter:

- default names are always available: `星芽` and `咚咚`;
- the child can rename them with parent help;
- gameplay text should still work if the names are changed;
- character roles should not depend on gender.

Name candidates:

| Role | Recommended | Other cute options |
| --- | --- | --- |
| high/right-hand dino | 星芽 | 跳跳, 亮亮, 咪咪, 小星 |
| low/left-hand triceratops | 咚咚 | 墩墩, 稳稳, 圆角角, 小鼓 |

## Story Arc

Canonical numbering is fixed across the project:

1. `月亮小家`
2. `星星桥`
3. `会听的小种子`
4. `咚咚的低音星球`
5. `会唱歌的大家园`

`音符护照` is a cross-chapter collection/rest system, not Chapter 4. It quietly collects icon-led story memories at natural rests; it is not a score ladder and its item count cannot mean mastery. Detailed teaching sequences and gates live in `03_CONTENT_ROADMAP.md`, `09_SCAFFOLD_AND_ASSESSMENT_RULES.md`, and `33_GAME_TEACHING_PACING_AND_MOTIVATION_CONTRACT.md`.

### Chapter 1: Moon Little Home

Working Chinese title: `月亮小家`

Purpose:

- introduce `Do/C`, `Re/D`, `Mi/E`, then `Fa/F`, `Sol/G`;
- establish keyboard location before staff reading becomes the main challenge;
- make each note visibly useful in the world.

Story:

星芽的小飞船降落在月亮上。这里不是它原本的家，而是建立太空新家园的第一处落脚点。外面没有空气，星芽穿着完整气密探索服；地板没落下，灯还没亮，车轮不见了，屋顶也没有合上。每一个音符都会帮一个小部件回到位置，屋顶闭合和压力灯亮起后，小家才成为安全前哨；为了马上去外部准备星星桥，第一、二章的游戏画面里星芽仍保持完整密封，不安排开盔再密封的重复换装。

Player emotion:

- "I am helping the little dinosaur build a home."
- "Each note makes something happen."

Pedagogy:

- explore -> follow -> compare -> check;
- color and glow are early helpers only;
- later passes must ask the child to use note name and keyboard locator.

### Chapter 2: Staff Star Bridge

Working Chinese title: `星星桥`

Purpose:

- make the five-line staff a physical place, not a worksheet;
- connect staff position to note name and key location;
- turn note reading into jumps.

Story:

月亮前哨亮起来以后，远处出现一颗可能适合长期生活的新星球。中间没有路，只有一段发光的五线谱。星芽保持完整密封，踩在每一个音符小垫上，一步一步穿过开放太空，到达对面的星球。这是一条供单个探索者通行的临时星光路线，不是第五章让两个住处长期相连的永久家园桥。

Player emotion:

- "The staff is a bridge."
- "A note on the staff tells the dinosaur where to jump."

Pedagogy:

- first pass: label + key locator + staff pad;
- second pass: softer cue, less glowing key;
- check pass: the bridge moves only when the child identifies the staff note.

### Chapter 3: Listening Seed

Working Chinese title: `会听的小种子`

Purpose:

- connect heard piano sound to note identity;
- make touch-only listening work before microphone/MIDI are required.

Story:

星芽抵达后先让星星背包检测空气。确认安全的短过场里，头盔自动掀开，外层压力服收好；随后星芽在花园里发现会发光的小种子。可见模仿时种子会唱出自己的音；隐藏听辨时改由中央的中性回声种核发声，所有候选植物在孩子回答前保持同样姿势。孩子找到同一个键后，对应种子才发芽或转向花朵。

Player emotion:

- "I heard a note and found it."
- "The sound is part of the story."

Pedagogy:

- a visible modeled note comes before small-set hidden calls;
- the child first echoes C, D, and E separately, then compares known two- and three-note sets;
- feedback names the note and locator;
- no target flower, route end, character gesture, stereo position, or distance animation identifies the hidden call before the response;
- later MIDI/microphone can feed the same loop;
- do not call this absolute pitch: every reduced-cue check keeps a learned reference or just-practiced candidate set.

### Chapter 4: Low Planet Friend

Working Chinese title: `咚咚的低音星球`

Purpose:

- introduce lower sounds, left-hand region, and later bass staff;
- keep the idea concrete before using technical terms.

Story:

星芽所在的生态星球脚下轻轻震了一下。孩子后来可以把这片温暖地下生态层昵称为“低音星球”，但它不是第三颗新星球。星芽先听见地底的“咚”，再找到一座圆圆的地下小屋。洞里的剪影慢慢露出眼睛、颈盾和脚印，最后才出现三角龙咚咚。咚咚的家需要更稳、更低的声音来修地基。

Player emotion:

- "Low notes feel heavy and strong."
- "Another dinosaur has a different kind of music job."

Pedagogy:

- do not overload Chapter 1 with left hand;
- introduce low/high through physical contrast first: jumpy/light vs steady/grounded;
- first find the low keyboard route, then let the left hand walk that familiar route, and only afterward place it on the bass staff;
- reconnect treble/right and bass/left maps in Chapter 5 rather than teaching all four labels at once.

### Chapter 5: Two Homes Become One

Working Chinese title: `会唱歌的大家园`

Purpose:

- prepare for two-hand coordination and grand staff;
- make cooperation the story reason for more complex music.

Story:

咚咚从地下生态层的地基站发出低音信号，星芽在可呼吸花园的中继台回答；两位角色都留在生态星球的安全控制区。远处月亮前哨是地图上的自动端点：较高的声音让远端桥灯和模块响应，较低的声音托住生态侧地基，临时探索路线因此升级成能够长期连接两个安全住处的双层家园桥。最终庆祝发生在花园中继台，不让无头盔角色跑到开放太空桥中央。

Player emotion:

- "Two dinosaurs can play together."
- "High and low sounds make a bigger world."

Pedagogy:

- right-hand/high notes and left-hand/low notes become meaningful because each character has a job;
- central C can become the meeting place;
- later grand staff becomes the map of the shared home.

Chapter 5 route rule:

- `TH01-TH05` is the core sequence. It flows directly into the `TH08` relay finale without asking the child to choose `easy` or `hard`.
- When two-note input is available and the child is comfortable, the bonus route inserts `TH06-TH07` before the `TH08` together finale.

## Chapter Transition Invariants

- Chapter 2 grows from Chapter 1 key geography; staff positions are introduced through a guided physical bridge, not a surprise test.
- Chapter 3 grows from M03's wheel echo and the sounds heard on the bridge.
- Chapter 4 begins because the completed LS08 root sends an unscored low echo underground. The child is not expected to find C3 yet; LP01 starts the actual high/low teaching. Dongdong is heard before being seen and seen before left-hand terminology appears.
- Chapter 5 begins only after Dongdong's low bridge sends a signal back to Xingya. Cooperation is the reason for hand alternation and grand staff.
- Each chapter introduces one new learning axis first, then reconnects it to prior note identity. New register, new clef, new hand, and new rhythm must never arrive together.
- A child can receive the story reward through the age-appropriate route even when a later mastery branch remains in progress.

## Child-Facing Copy Style

Use very short lines. The character bubble should carry the hint.

Good:

- ordinary UI: `找 C。`
- ordinary UI: `两个黑键左边。`
- dinosaur bubble: `我唱 Do，你来弹 C。`
- `跳到这颗音符星。`
- `听一听，是哪颗音？`
- ordinary repair: `刚才是 D。再找 C。`
- dinosaur repair: `刚才是 Re；我要 Do，你来弹 C。`

Avoid:

- ordinary child-facing `Do/C` dual labels or visible solfege outside the dinosaur bubble;
- long instruction paragraphs;
- repeated top-panel text;
- abstract text like "build staff recognition ability";
- adult explanations in the child play area.

## Level Design Pattern

Every level should have this rhythm:

1. A visible problem appears.
2. The character reacts to the problem.
3. The note identity appears.
4. The key or staff clue appears.
5. Correct input changes the world immediately.
6. Wrong input gives a concrete comparison.
7. The level ends by creating the reason for the next level.

Example:

- Problem: the moon rover has no wheel.
- Note identity: `Re/D`.
- Locator: middle white key in the two-black group.
- Correct: wheel snaps on.
- Next reason: the rover rolls to the broken bridge.

## Teaching Scaffold By Story

| Phase | Story feeling | UI cue | Assessment rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Explore | "What does this note do?" | strong glow, name, locator | wrong is harmless discovery |
| Follow | "Can I help in order?" | sequence path, labels | child follows short pattern |
| Compare | "Which one is different?" | two or three nearby choices | wrong feedback names the pressed note |
| Read | "Where does the dino jump?" | staff pad + note identity | key glow reduced after first pass |
| Check | "Can I do it myself?" | minimal glow, story consequence | mastery requires reduced-cue success |

## Visual And Audio Motifs

Reusable motifs:

- moon floor tiles;
- sleepy lights;
- snap-in wheels;
- toy bridge boards;
- star gates;
- note pads on staff lines/spaces;
- seed sprouts for listening;
- high planet: brighter, light jumps;
- low planet: warmer, rounder, stronger footstep animations.

Audio cues:

- correct key: clean piano tone + tiny world sound;
- wrong key: soft wooden wobble, never harsh buzz;
- completed sequence: small musical flourish using only learned notes;
- staff jump: light landing sound synced with the note.

## Do Not Do

- Do not introduce both dinosaurs before the child understands the first keyboard area.
- Do not make Dongdong a "slow" or "wrong" character.
- Do not make color the solution in later levels.
- Do not make the staff bridge a flat quiz panel.
- Do not use copyrighted character shapes, names, melodies, or recognizable franchise themes.
- Do not add too many words for a 4-6 year old to read.

## Design Acceptance Gate

A story level is acceptable only if all answers are yes:

- Can a child tell what changed after pressing the right note?
- Does the story explain why this note matters?
- Does the level teach at least two identity links, such as note name + key locator?
- If the child presses wrong, does the app tell what was pressed and what to try?
- Can the level still work without MIDI or microphone?
- Is the scene cute and readable without adult explanation?
