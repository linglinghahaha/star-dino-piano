# Business Wedge And App Store Story

## Market Wedge

Do not position this as a general "learn piano fast" app. Established competitors are stronger there.

Position it as:

> A story-based first piano note trainer for young children before full song practice.

## Buyer

Primary payer:

- parent of a 4-8 year-old beginner.

Secondary recommender:

- piano teacher who wants a playful note-reading and key-finding supplement.

## First App Store Promise

The first App Store promise should be narrow and believable:

- learn the first piano notes;
- find notes on real-looking piano keys;
- read the first staff positions through a dinosaur bridge;
- use touch first, with MIDI/microphone as optional growth paths.

Avoid:

- "master piano";
- "learn any song";
- "AI piano teacher";
- "works with every keyboard";
- "perfect microphone recognition."

## Three Screenshot Story

1. `Dino jumps on the staff bridge`
   - Shows the unique mechanic.
2. `Do/C on a real piano-like keyboard`
   - Shows the teaching link.
3. `Parent view: today's notes`
   - Shows adult trust and educational value.

Later screenshots can show MIDI/microphone, but only after those paths are reliable enough.

## Retention Loop

Retention should come from:

- unlocking more note passports;
- reaching new planets;
- replaying short daily challenges;
- parent seeing concrete learning progress;
- child recognizing the dinosaur and world.

Do not rely on an endless song catalog for retention in the MVP.

## Differentiation From Close Competitors

Compared with song-first piano apps:

- narrower;
- younger;
- concept-first;
- story-first.

Compared with worksheet-style note reading:

- more embodied;
- the staff is a bridge;
- correct notes move a character.

Compared with generic music games:

- tied to real piano key layout;
- uses solfege, letter names, staff positions, and sound together.

Current competitive implication, 2026-07-10:

- Simply Piano, flowkey, Yousician, and Piano Maestro are too strong on broad lessons and song practice to fight directly.
- Note Rush and Mussila show that note-reading and child music games are already real categories.
- The wedge must be more specific: pre-reading-friendly staff bridge + piano key locator + note identity + sound, not just "cute note practice."

## MVP Monetization Hypothesis

Possible future model:

- free first chapter;
- paid full chapter pack;
- no ads in child experience;
- no manipulative reward pressure.

This should be tested later. It should not influence the current prototype before the core learning loop is strong.
