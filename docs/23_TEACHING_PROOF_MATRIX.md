## Evidence Time Scale

- `played` proves that the child completed a guided story action.
- `stable` proves current-period reduced-cue performance under the family threshold in `09`.
- `retained` requires a different session on a different local calendar date, at least 8 hours after the qualifying stable run, followed by a reduced-cue retrieval that meets the family threshold. The browser event/scheduler implementation exists at `334g`; physical-device timing behavior, teacher review and learning-effect evidence remain unproven.
- The product may continue the story after `played`, but parent copy must not turn `stable` into a long-term learning claim.
- Retention gate evidence must record the earlier session, later review time/session id, cue strength, wrong inputs and result; `334g` implements these browser events, while physical-device timing and learning-effect evidence remain missing.

Do not put the whole product proof slice into one 3-5 minute child session. It changes from note identity to listening to route memory to staff reading too quickly. Use three separate short observations, each with one main teaching axis.

This section schedules the first-course content only. `docs/37` is authoritative for guardian information, privacy, adult scripts, intervention levels, stopping, samples and release retest.

Future `C3-X01`, LP05+ and Chapter 5 scheduling remain missing, as do physical-iPad behavior and child-usability proof.

Observation standard:

- one child is a directional usability signal only;
- do not claim low-age readiness until 3-5 children have completed the relevant observation sessions after the current visual/audio pass;
- every adult hint must be logged, especially pointing, reading, or explaining the target;
- rerun the observation after final art/audio/CSS changes because children may follow different visual anchors.

1. **Observation A, keyboard home (3-5 minutes):** `M01`, then the C-D-E lamp actions in `M02`. Stop when the three lamps settle.
2. **Observation B, listening (about 3 minutes):** one short known-key warm-up, then the two modeled calls in `M03`. Stop when the wheel reaches its bay.
3. **Observation C, staff bridge (3-5 minutes):** a brief C-D-E keyboard reminder, then `S01-mini`. Jump two or three staff pads and stop at the visible rest star.

`M08` and `FG03` remain required proof levels. The guided M08 construction route may be observed in canonical course order before the dedicated F/G lessons, but it proves only whether the child understands the five-step construction and first full route. Its reduced-cue seal check, color-reduced use and any F/G locator claim are observed only after `FG01-FG03`; one guided M08 pass never proves F/G stable.

This observation series is not the same as full S01 mastery. Observation C checks whether a young child can understand the staff-bridge mechanic in a short session. Full S01 mastery still requires the complete C-G bridge and reduced-cue/staff-check conditions above. The mini route does not write stable S01 mastery or auto-enter `staff-check`.

Pass condition:

- child knows where to look after the screen loads;
- child can press the screen keyboard without adult pointing at every note;
- one wrong input produces a recoverable, non-scary teaching moment;
- the child sees a clear story result;
- the session ends without needing a manual close/next-button explanation.
- the observer can describe which cue actually guided the child: sound, dino motion, staff pad, black-key mini-map, key geometry, or text.
